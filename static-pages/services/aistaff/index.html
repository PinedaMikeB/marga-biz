FAQ

Is this just a chatbot?

No. AIStaff qualifies leads using your sales rules, saves structured data to a dashboard, prepares quotation drafts, and tracks follow-up—not generic FAQ replies only.

Will AI send quotations automatically?

No. By default, AI prepares a draft and your admin approves before anything official is sent to the customer.

Can a human take over?

Yes. Your team can jump into Messenger anytime; AIStaff keeps context and conversation history in the dashboard.

Do we need a website?

No—Messenger-first. Many clients start with Facebook Page inquiries only. Website chat can be added on Pro.
