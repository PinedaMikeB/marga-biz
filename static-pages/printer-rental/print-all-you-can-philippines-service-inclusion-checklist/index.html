Service inclusions to clarify before comparing price

  • Expected monthly pages, peak days, and number of users.
  • Whether toner, ink, parts, and scheduled maintenance are included.
  • How urgent service calls, temporary replacement units, and downtime are handled.
  • Whether the printer must support scanning, copying, color output, or network sharing.
  • Which city page should support the quote: Makati, BGC, Taguig, Manila, Pasig, Quezon City, or Ortigas.

How to decide if a higher-volume package is justified

A standard printer rental may be enough for a small admin team with predictable low output. A Print All You Can discussion becomes more useful when printing is recurring, multiple departments share one machine, or the team wants one support path instead of separate consumable and repair decisions.
