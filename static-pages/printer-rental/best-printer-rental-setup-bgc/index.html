Best Printer Rental Setup for BGC Offices and Startups

A BGC support guide for choosing the right office printer setup

The best printer rental setup for BGC offices and startups depends on pace, headcount, document mix, and workspace style. A coworking tenant in Bonifacio Global City has a different requirement from a larger commercial office near High Street or Uptown.

This guide is written for teams in BGC, Bonifacio Global City, and Taguig that want a practical way to choose the right rental setup. BGC teams usually care about

  • Flexible monthly cost while the team is still growing.
  • A compact setup that fits startup and shared-office layouts.
  • Reliable support when the office cannot afford downtime.
  • Color output for proposals, decks, and presentations.

Startups should define first

  • How many people will share the printer.
  • Whether daily printing is mostly mono or mixed with color.
  • If scanning and copying are part of the workflow.
  • Whether the setup needs room to scale within a few months.

What printer setup fits each BGC scenario

Compact office printer

Works well for smaller startups, executive rooms, and low-to-moderate document output in limited office space.

Multifunction shared machine

Useful for coworking suites, operations teams, and offices that need print, scan, and copy from one device.

Color-capable department printer

Better for business-development, client-service, and management teams that present often and need cleaner color output.

High-volume office package

Right for larger BGC teams that need a stronger duty cycle than a small office printer can handle.

How BGC startups should choose the right rental plan

1

Map the actual users

Count the staff who will use the printer regularly, not just the size of the whole company.

2

Check the document mix

A startup printing contracts, decks, and onboarding forms has a different requirement from a team printing only internal reports.

3

Plan for growth

If headcount or document volume will change quickly, choose a setup that can scale instead of replacing the unit too soon.

4

Match the workspace style

BGC offices in shared, managed, or compact spaces usually need a cleaner footprint and practical network access.

Where BGC teams usually make the wrong decision

The common mistake is choosing a unit that is too small because the initial team is small. In BGC and Taguig, teams often grow fast or print more client-facing material than expected. A better decision is to choose based on the next stage of demand, not just the first week of use. BGC office scenarios and the better fit

Coworking or managed office tenant

Usually needs a compact shared unit with practical network use and less wasted floor space.

Startup with sales and operations teams

Often needs a multifunction unit that handles internal documents plus the occasional client-facing output.

Commercial office floor with shared departments

Should consider a stronger department-level machine instead of overloading a small office printer.

Presentation-heavy leadership team

May need reliable color capability and cleaner output for proposals, reports, and investor or client meetings.

What to confirm before you approve a BGC printer setup

Before you approve a BGC or Taguig rental setup, confirm the monthly volume, number of users, need for mono versus color output, and what support is included after installation. That makes it easier to choose a setup that keeps up with the office instead of one that feels cheap for only the first month.
