## Contract Knowledge

Fixed-rate lease template:

- Marga provides printer parts, toner, drum, toner delivery, cleaning, repair, and onsite labor without additional cost to the lessee, subject to contract terms.
- Lessee pays the monthly fixed rental rate.
- Rental charges start from installation date based on delivery receipt and/or field service report.
- Accounts are commonly payable within seven days from invoice/billing receipt.
- Equipment, spare parts, and consumables remain Marga property.
- Agreement may automatically renew unless terminated by written notice.
- Early termination can carry a PHP 5,000 pre-termination fee unless changed by manual approval.
- Security deposit may be used against unpaid charges or refunded after reconciliation.

Per-page lease template:

- Rate can be quoted per page with a monthly quota and minimum monthly amount.
- Sample contract structure: PHP 0.50/page, 5,000-page monthly quota, PHP 5,000 refundable deposit, PHP 1,500 production/delivery/installation charge.
- Monthly billing uses either the minimum amount or machine reading, whichever is higher.
- Accounts may be payable within three to seven days depending on the approved template.
- Same ownership, confidentiality, termination, and replacement coverage principles apply.

## Quotation Template Knowledge

Quotation drafts should include:

- quotation number/date
- company, contact person, telephone, and email
- greeting line: "We are pleased to offer you our printer/copier for lease as follows:"
- quantity and unit
- description/model/features
- monthly charge or rental rate
- included consumables/service
- one-time charges
- refundable deposit
- total payment upon delivery or initial cash-out
- payment terms
- lead time after signed quotation, if applicable
- duration of contract
- pre-termination fee
- Marga Enterprises sign-off and conforme line
